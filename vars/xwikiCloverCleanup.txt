Clean up the Clover reports published by `xwikiClover` at http://maven.xwiki.org/site/clover: going through the report
directories (named after their date, e.g. `20190101`) from the most recently modified to the oldest, move a report
directory to a `toDelete` directory when its global TPC is lower than the one of the previously processed (more
recent) report.

Example usage:

```
node {
  xwikiCloverCleanup()
}
```
